Commerce

A site this size gets away from you. There were three products to speak for, and pages long enough that a reader loses the thread partway down. So we stopped drawing pages and built pieces instead, a handful of them, made once and used again wherever they fit. A rounded face for the headlines, a plain one for the rest, indigo underneath, and lime held back for the one place that has to be seen.
