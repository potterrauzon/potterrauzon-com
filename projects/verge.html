Verge · WEB APP

During an incident, which is the one time it truly matters, the dashboard flickered and reflowed and handed the on-call engineer a wall of noise with nothing to tell one part of it from another.

I gave the layout fixed zones that hold still, and rendered the stream on a canvas so it never reflows underneath you. Severity is the only thing allowed to bring in color. I built the live components to batch their updates, on the simple grounds that the eye has a limit.
